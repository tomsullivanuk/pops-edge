About these prices

Pregame, regular-season game winners only. Prices stop being current after five minutes or at scheduled start, whichever comes first. Opening, filtering and switching sports never retrieves new odds. Missing prices are not zero. This sheet does not show holdings, model-derived edges or betting recommendations.

Refresh scope, source evidence and recovery

Refresh retrieves the official schedule and market catalog, then supported team YES books for the selected day. Team and completion filters change the view only. Each contract keeps its own capture time. Failed books remain missing; a failed schedule or catalog keeps the previous saved sheet with its original dates. Verify the local clock before restarting after a clock warning. If an attempt was interrupted, check that no other app is refreshing, then start a new manual refresh. No research data is repaired.
