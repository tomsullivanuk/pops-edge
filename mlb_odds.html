About these prices

Saved prices are pregame observations for regular-season game winners. They are not guaranteed available or closing prices. Missing prices are not zero. Game results are separate from Kalshi settlement, payout and profit. No model comparison is available.

Technical evidence and recovery

Refresh MLB sheet updates the official schedule and game results for the selected date, then retrieves eligible pregame prices. For a past saved date, it updates results and retains earlier captured prices. Saved prices retain their original capture dates; current-price eligibility still ends after five minutes or at scheduled start. The date selects the official MLB schedule; Today and Tomorrow follow Central Time. Team and completion filters change the view only. Each contract keeps its own capture time. Failed books supply no new price; a compatible previous capture may remain visible with its original date; a failed official schedule keeps the previous sheet. If market discovery fails, valid game results can update while odds remain unavailable and earlier compatible captures keep their original dates. Verify the local clock before restarting after a clock warning. If an attempt was interrupted, check that no other app is refreshing, then start a new manual refresh. No research data is repaired.
