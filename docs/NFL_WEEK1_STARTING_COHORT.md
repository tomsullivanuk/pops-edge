# NFL 2026 partial Week 1 starting cohort

Owner-approved exception, September 12, 2026. This addendum governs only the
initial 2026 Week 1 cohort; the full-week protocol remains unchanged for Week 2
onward and for installations without explicit partial-cohort commissioning.
Its exact digest and the original official schedule are pinned at activation.
Existing initialized stores are not silently migrated.

The cohort consists of the 14 official Week 1 games that have not started at
commissioning. The two exclusions must be NE at SEA and SF at LAR. Record all
16 official identities, fixed membership, the actual effective time and the raw
schedule evidence. Reject initialization with an incomplete/ambiguous schedule,
unknown kickoff, an additional started game, or a different excluded pair.
No arbitrary owner-selected exclusions or automatic rolling cohort are allowed.

Use the latest valid ELWAY workbook imported after activation and a fresh Kalshi
capture in that workflow. Existing workbook publication time remains original;
import/capture times are actual. Do not reuse archived prices or fabricate an
old import. Freeze at the earliest kickoff among the fixed 14 games. Every
selected quote must finish strictly before that common cutoff. Normal version
selection, immutable first quotes, pre-cutoff missing-only retries, corrections,
fee-free scoring and paired coverage rules continue to apply. An earlier
corrected start can invalidate observations; a closed window cannot reopen.

The two original exclusions remain visible and never receive a score. A later
schedule change cannot add a replacement game to the fixed cohort. Missing
members/unknown times prevent asserting a known cutoff. Preserve the official
16-game denominator and separately expose the 14-game eligible denominator;
missing market/forecast data can reduce usable coverage below 14. Do not claim
14 successful captures merely because 14 games are eligible.

Label reports and refresh status “Partial Week 1 — 14 of 16 games”. Eventual
cumulative views must retain that label and disclose the partial starting week.
This is collection before the included games, not a full-week baseline captured
before the season opener. The older 16-game historical comparison is not enrolled.
No fees, profit/loss, wagering-policy authority or MLB behavior changes follow.

Accepted limitations: manual collection, visible partial or failed acquisitions,
no automatic retries, and no results tab in this implementation slice. Source
installation is separate from deployment and actual commissioning. The owner’s
approval of this starting cohort supplies the methodological decision; each
operational action must carry its applicable authorization.
