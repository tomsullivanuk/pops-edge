## Release outcome

Trustworthy MLB Kalshi collection, complete and honest evidence accounting,
reproducible separate study reports and useful local presentation. **Performance
Report** is the main live-quote page, with a compact link to a static **Historical
candle report** and separately dated collection status. NFL v1.1 and World Cup
remain intact. A favorable result is not an acceptance condition.

The historical programme in `RELEASE_PLAN_v1.1.0.md` remains traceability and
scientific-dependency documentation, not the current whole-release checklist.
The narrowed release does not require external supplier admission, native-model
success, PR18/PR19 closure, applicability/policy work, the broader Workspace,
automated wagering or hosting migration. Unified UX remains tentative v1.3 work.

## Must hold, accepted limitations and deferred improvements

Must hold: immutable Evidence, truthful chronology, exact source/analysis replay,
complete failure-inclusive denominators, separate studies, preserved study windows,
visible report-generation failures and useful local delivery. The initial reference
is the approved constant 0.5 descriptive baseline only. Each report retains its
own observation interval, source cutoff, generation time, rules and provenance.

Accepted: local/manual operation, visibly missed observations, incomplete but
honestly labelled studies, insufficient samples, frozen status, and documented
safe manual recovery. A release may show in-progress reports, but must deliver
actual validated report capability for both studies, not unexplained placeholders.
Full-window retrospective acquisition and prospective closure are not falsely
claimed by that release decision. Existing final-report obligations remain due
under their original gates.

Deferred: automatic refresh/recovery, servers/shared shell, arbitrary windows,
fitted baselines or additional statistics, cloud operation and all excluded
supplier/model/policy/wagering work above. The usual independent review, amendment
and release-readiness review budget applies; P2/P3 refinements do not become new
must-hold conditions without concrete evidence of a governing violation.
